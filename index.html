The story
of CR7.

Cristiano Ronaldo's journey began on the island of Madeira, Portugal, and grew into one of football's most remarkable careers.

As a teenager, Ronaldo left home to pursue football at Sporting CP in Lisbon. His speed, skill, and determination soon caught the world's attention. In 2003, he joined Manchester United, where he developed into a global star.

He went on to play for Real Madrid and Juventus, winning major trophies along the way. With Portugal, he helped his country win the 2016 European Championship and the 2019 UEFA Nations League.

His story is a reminder: talent opens a door, but dedication keeps you moving forward.

Player stories

Argentina / Forward

Lionel Messi

Growing up in Rosario, Argentina, Messi joined Barcelona's youth academy at 13. He made his first-team debut in 2004 and became one of the club's greatest players before later playing for Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami.

With Argentina, he won the Copa América in 2021 and captained his country to the 2022 FIFA World Cup. His journey is a story of close control, creativity, and a lifelong love of the game.

Portugal / Defender

Pepe

Born in Brazil, Pepe moved to Portugal as a teenager to pursue football. He began his senior career with Marítimo, then became a standout defender at Porto and Real Madrid, known for his strength, focus, and determination.

He won major club trophies, including three UEFA Champions League titles with Real Madrid, and helped Portugal win the 2016 European Championship. After a long career, he announced his retirement from professional football in 2024.
